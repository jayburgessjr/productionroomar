ProductionRoomAR privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

ProductionRoomAR is a virtual movie theater for Meta Quest where you screen your own films. It is built so that your work stays yours.

What we collect

Nothing. ProductionRoomAR has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics and no advertising. We never receive your videos, your viewing history or any personal information.

Files on your headset

When you choose a folder, the app reads the videos and subtitle files in it directly on your headset, using the system folder picker. Resume positions, favorites and settings are stored only on your headset.

Dropbox (optional)

If you sign in to Dropbox, you sign in on Dropbox's own page in the headset browser. The app keeps the resulting access token encrypted on your headset and uses it only to list and stream videos from your Dropbox straight to your headset. Signing out deletes the token from the headset. Dropbox's handling of your data is covered by Dropbox's privacy policy.

Frame captures

Frames you capture are saved to your headset's Pictures folder and go nowhere else.

Meta

Meta Horizon OS and the Meta Horizon Store may collect data under Meta's privacy policy. ProductionRoomAR does not add to it.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the ProductionRoomAR page on the Meta Horizon Store.
