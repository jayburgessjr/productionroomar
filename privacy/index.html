ProductionRoomAR privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

ProductionRoomAR is a virtual movie theater for Meta Quest where you screen your own films. It is built so that your work stays yours.

What we collect

Nothing. ProductionRoomAR has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics and no advertising. We never receive your videos, your notes, your voice recordings or your viewing history. The app sends us no personal information.

Files on your headset

When you choose a folder, the app reads the videos and subtitle files in it directly on your headset, using the system folder picker. Resume positions, favorites and settings are stored only on your headset.

Thumbnails and previews

To show your library, the app keeps small thumbnails and seek-bar preview images of your videos in its private cache on your headset. The app removes old ones as that library's list of videos changes, though not while the list is empty. Signing out of Dropbox removes all of them, and so does uninstalling the app.

Dropbox (optional)

If you sign in to Dropbox, you sign in on Dropbox's own page in the headset browser. The app keeps the resulting access token encrypted on your headset and uses it only to list your videos, stream them and make their thumbnails and previews, straight from Dropbox to your headset. Signing out deletes the token from the headset and asks Dropbox to revoke it. You can also remove the app in your Dropbox settings under Connected apps. Dropbox's handling of your data is covered by Dropbox's privacy policy.

Frame captures

Frames you capture are saved to your headset's Pictures folder, in ProductionRoomAR Screenshots. The app sends them nowhere else.

Review notes and voice notes

Notes you take are stored only on your headset. A voice note uses your headset's microphone, and only while you hold the voice note button. The recording is saved privately inside the app, and it is turned into text by your headset's own on-device speech recognition, so the audio never leaves your headset. Deleting a note deletes its recording. Notes and recordings leave the app only when you export them, which saves them to your headset's Documents folder, in ProductionRoom. Exported files and frame captures sit in shared storage, so other apps you allow to read your files can see them, and they stay until you delete them.

Links and YouTube

If you paste a link, the app plays the video straight from that address, so the site that hosts it sees the request as it would from any video player. Your last five links are kept on your headset. The full address is encrypted, and only the site name and title shown in the list are not. A YouTube link plays in YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed (youtube-nocookie.com), so YouTube handles that playback under Google's privacy policy. While it plays, YouTube can set cookies and store data in the player. The app clears that data when you leave the video or close the app. If the app is stopped abruptly, the data stays in the app's private storage until you next open and leave a YouTube video, or uninstall the app.

Deleting your data

We hold none of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete. Everything is on your headset, and you can remove it yourself:

  • A note or voice note: delete the note in the app. Its recording goes with it.
  • Recent links: press Clear in the Link tab.
  • Dropbox: sign out in the app.
  • Everything stored inside the app: uninstall it.
  • Exported notes, recordings and frame captures: delete them in your headset's Files app. Deleting a note or uninstalling the app does not remove these copies.

Meta

Meta Horizon OS and the Meta Horizon Store may collect data under Meta's privacy policy. ProductionRoomAR does not add to it.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
